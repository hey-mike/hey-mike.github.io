Enterprise Trading Architecture Transformation at oOh!media

Architected and delivered a distributed trading ecosystem and real-time data pipelines, defining the technical strategy for a modernised media booking platform.

A monolithic trading platform struggling under peak campaign demand, with rigid data flows between booking, scheduling, and reporting that made even small platform changes high-risk.

Solution

Defined technical vision and reference architecture, implementing domain-driven design with event-sourced microservices and real-time analytics capabilities.

Outcomes

01

Increased booking throughput during peak demand periods

02

Reduced campaign setup time from days to hours with automated pipelines

03

Improved infrastructure efficiency while increasing resilience
