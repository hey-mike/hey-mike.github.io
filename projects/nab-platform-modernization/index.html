NAB Platform Integration for the Citi Acquisition

Led the technical vision for integrating NAB's digital banking platform with the acquired Citi consumer portfolio, aligning architecture with acquisition timelines and regulatory requirements.

Challenge

Merging two major banking platforms post-acquisition without service disruption, under tight regulatory timelines and with teams that had never worked together before.

Solution

Defined the integration architecture using micro-frontend composition, established shared delivery pipelines across both organisations, and held technical governance across the combined team.

Outcomes

01

Helped accelerate the acquisition timeline through platform integration delivered on schedule

02

Maintained strong availability during critical migration phases

03

Enabled cross-brand functionality across a large consumer transaction landscape
